*TeacherName* is generally the last name of the teachers in this school. It is used to uniquely identify each teacher in the database. No two teachers can have the same name in the system, which a possible reason why a teacher in this system is listed as their first name. Other acceptable formats for the name could be a combination of their first and last name with a space in between. As well as their first initial followed by their last name. If the last name appears first, it is to be separated by a comma from the first name when keyed into the system. 

Due to potential computer system requirements and limitations these names are limited to 15 characters each, spaces included.
