The office is the room or location that a teacher will most likely be found in at the school. This represents the same information as [[Room]] but does not indicated where a teacher is at each class. For example, the gym teacher could also teach a health class in room B29 for one period. 

It is stored as up to 6 characters (letters) in the database.
