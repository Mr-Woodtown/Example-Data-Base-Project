The lunch slot that this teacher has been assigned to take during the day. This also determines the start and ending times of the 3rd and 7th periods of the day for students in a teacher's class. 

This is one of the whole numbers 1, 2, or 0. 0 represents an unassigned or not applicable lunch slot.
